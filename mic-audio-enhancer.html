How it works

One chain of audio filters — the same kind of processing a podcast editor applies by hand.

Remove rumble and hiss

Low-frequency rumble (AC, fan, desk bumps) and high-frequency hiss are filtered out.

Boost voice clarity

The voice's presence frequencies (2–5kHz) get a gentle boost so speech sounds sharp and clear.

Even out the volume

Compression and loudness normalization smooth out the ups and downs — YouTube-ready loudness.

Your privacy is respected. Processing happens entirely inside your browser using WebAssembly — your files are never uploaded or sent to any server.
