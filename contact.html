What happens after you email us

A quick look at how messages get handled, so you know what to expect.

1

Your email lands in one inbox

There's no ticket system or bot in between — every message goes straight to the person who builds and maintains the apps and tools.

2

Bug reports are checked first

If something is broken for you, it's likely broken for others too, so issue reports with clear steps are usually looked at before general questions.

3

You get a reply, not a auto-response

Replies are written individually rather than pulled from a canned template, which is part of why responses can take a day or two rather than being instant.

4

Good suggestions can turn into real features

If a suggestion is useful and fits the direction of the studio, it can end up on the roadmap for a future update.
