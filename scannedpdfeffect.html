How it works

Three steps, all running locally in your browser tab.

Add your PDF

Drag and drop a PDF onto the drop zone, or click Choose PDF to browse your device.

Pick a style and tweak the sliders

Choose black & white, sepia or color, then adjust grain, contrast and tilt until it looks right.

Download your scanned-look PDF

Every page is rebuilt into a fresh PDF you can save straight to your device.

Why give a PDF a scanned look

A few common reasons people reach for this effect.

Matching archived paperwork

Blend a freshly typed page in with older documents that were genuinely scanned or photocopied.

Design & mockups

Give a poster, prop or presentation slide an authentic "paper" texture instead of a flat digital look.

Testing document tools

Generate realistic scan-style samples to test OCR, archiving or document-management software.

Creative & print projects

Add a worn, analog feel to zines, collages or vintage-styled layouts.

Your privacy is respected. Every page is rendered and processed entirely inside your browser — your PDF is never uploaded or sent to any server.
