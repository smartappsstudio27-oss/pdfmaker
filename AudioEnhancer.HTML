Ye kaam kaise karta hai

Ek chain of audio filters use hoti hai — bilkul waise hi jaise podcast editors manually apply karte hain.

01

Rumble aur Hiss Hatana

Low-frequency rumble (AC, fan, table-hit) aur high-frequency hiss ko filter karke nikala jaata hai.

02

Voice Clarity Boost

Voice ki presence frequencies (2-5kHz) ko thoda boost kiya jaata hai taaki bolne wala sharp aur clear sunayi de.

03

Volume Consistent Karna

Compression aur loudness normalization se volume ke ups-downs balance ho jaate hain — YouTube-ready loudness.
