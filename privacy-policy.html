Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to SmartApps Studio — here's exactly what we collect, why, and how it's handled across our website, browser tools and Android apps.

01 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how SmartApps Studio collects, uses and protects information across our website (smartappsstudio.in), our free browser-based tools, and our Android applications and games.

02 Browser-Based Tools

Tools like Mic Audio Enhancer, MP4 to MP3 Converter and Online PDF Maker run entirely inside your browser using WebAssembly. Any file you select — video, audio, image or document — is processed locally on your own device and is never uploaded to our servers or any third party. When you close the tab or refresh the page, the file and its output are gone; we never had a copy of it.

In short: what you drop into a tool never leaves your device.

03 Information We Collect

Depending on the app or page, we may collect limited technical information such as device type, browser type, app version, crash reports and anonymous analytics (via Google Analytics) to help us understand usage and improve performance. We do not require an account or personal sign-up to use any tool on this website.

04 Advertising

This website displays advertisements using Google AdSense, and our Android applications may display ads using Google AdMob. These ad providers may use cookies or device identifiers to serve relevant ads and may collect information as described in their own privacy policies. You can learn more about how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

05 App Permissions

Our Android applications request only the permissions required for their core functionality — for example, camera or storage access for an app that scans documents. We do not request permissions unrelated to an app's purpose.

06 Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information that is collected. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

07 Children's Privacy

Our apps, tools and website are not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through any of our products, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

08 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as we add new tools or apps. Any changes will be posted on this page along with an updated revision date at the top.
