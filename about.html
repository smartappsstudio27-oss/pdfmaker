Our Story

How it started

SmartApps Studio started with a simple goal: build Android apps and browser tools that are genuinely useful, without the bloat, clutter or invasive permissions that most apps come with today.

The first release was PDF Maker — a straightforward tool for turning photos into clean, private PDFs. Since then the studio has grown to include free browser-based tools like MP4 to MP3 Converter and Mic Audio Enhancer, all built around the same idea: your files stay on your device, and the tool does one job really well.

It's a small, independent operation — one developer designing, building and shipping every app and tool on this site. That keeps things focused: no growth hacks, no dark patterns, just steady, useful updates.
