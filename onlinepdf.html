Why PDF

Why turn images into a PDF in the first place?

A loose collection of photos is hard to manage — separate files, inconsistent sizing, and awkward to attach to an email or form. A single PDF keeps every page in order, opens the same way on any device, and is the format most schools, banks, and government portals expect for document uploads. Converting on-device also means there's no waiting for a slow upload, and no copy of a sensitive document sitting on someone else's server afterward.

Common Uses

What people use PDF Maker for

🪪 ID documents — Aadhaar, PAN Card, Voter ID

Most online forms and portals ask for a single PDF of your ID rather than separate front/back photos. Since nothing is uploaded to a server here, it's a straightforward way to combine both sides into one clean file for a job application, bank KYC, or government form.

📝 Marksheets and certificates

Combine multiple pages of a marksheet, degree certificate, or transcript into one PDF for college admissions, scholarship applications, or job portals that only accept a single file per document.

🧾 Bills and receipts

Turn a stack of photographed receipts or utility bills into one PDF for expense reports, warranty claims, or tax record-keeping — much easier to file and search later than loose image files.

📚 Notes and handwritten pages

Students photographing handwritten notes or worksheets can combine several pages into a single, orderly PDF to share with classmates or keep as a study reference.

Yes. It's completely free to use in your browser, with no signup and no watermark.

No. The PDF is built entirely inside your browser using JavaScript, so your images never leave your device.

Is there an Android app version?

Yes. PDF Maker is available as a free Android app on the Google Play Store, with more features than the web tool.

How many images can I add at once?

You can add up to 20 images in one go, and remove any of them before creating the PDF.

Can I use this to convert Aadhaar or PAN card photos to PDF?

Yes. Since images never leave your device, it's a practical way to turn ID documents like Aadhaar or PAN card photos into a single PDF without uploading sensitive images anywhere.

What page sizes are supported?

You can choose A4, Letter, or a page size that automatically fits each image's original dimensions.

No. The generated PDF is completely clean, with no watermark or branding added to the file.
