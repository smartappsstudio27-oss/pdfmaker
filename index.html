About the studio

Small tools, built with care, by one person

SmartApps Studio started as a simple idea: most apps and websites try to do too much, and end up doing everything badly. Instead of building one giant app packed with features nobody uses, this studio focuses on small, single-purpose tools — each one built to solve exactly one problem, and to solve it well.

Every browser-based tool on this site — the PDF Maker, the MP4 to MP3 Converter, and the Mic Audio Enhancer — runs entirely inside your own browser using WebAssembly technology. That means your photos, videos, and audio files are processed directly on your device and never uploaded to any server. There's no waiting for uploads, no privacy risk, and no account required to get started.

On the Android side, apps like PDF Maker and Smart Typing follow the same philosophy: light on permissions, fast to open, and free of the clutter that slows down most mobile apps today. New tools and updates are added regularly as the studio grows, with every release focused on being genuinely useful in everyday life — whether that's turning a stack of photos into a PDF for a job application, cleaning up a noisy voice recording, or typing faster on a phone keyboard.

How these browser tools actually work

Mic Audio Enhancer uses on-device audio processing to detect and reduce steady background noise — fans, traffic, room hiss — while keeping the speaking voice clear. It's built for people recording voice notes, podcasts, or short videos on a phone mic in a less-than-perfect room, and works directly in the browser with no software to install.

MP4 to MP3 Converter strips the audio track out of a video file and saves it as a standalone MP3, without re-uploading anything to a server. It supports batch conversion of up to five files at once, which is handy for pulling audio out of lecture recordings, interviews, or saved reels.

Online PDF Maker turns one or more photos — ID documents, marksheets, bills, receipts — into a single clean PDF file, in the order you choose. Because everything happens on-device, it's a practical option for handling documents like Aadhaar or PAN card copies without uploading sensitive images anywhere.
