How it works

All running locally in your browser tab — nothing is ever uploaded.

Add your PDF files

Drag and drop one or more PDFs, including any that are password-protected.

Enter passwords and set the order

Type each file's password, or use "same password" if they all match. Use the arrows to arrange the final order.

Merge and download

Click Merge PDFs and download the single combined file, ready to use immediately.

What people use this for

A few common situations where unlocking and merging PDFs together saves real time.

Bank statements & salary slips

Combine several months of password-protected statements or slips into one file for a loan or visa application.

Scanned ID documents

Merge multiple locked PDFs — Aadhaar, marksheets, certificates — into a single, clean document.

Office reports

Combine password-protected reports from different departments into one file for easier review.

Reordering pages

Use the up/down arrows to fix the page order across files before creating the final merged PDF.

Your privacy is respected. Unlocking and merging happens entirely inside your browser — your PDFs and their passwords are never sent to any server.
