FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Images to PDF tool free?

Yes. It's completely free to use in your browser, with no signup and no watermark.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is built entirely inside your browser using JavaScript, so your images never leave your device.

Is there an Android app version?

Yes. PDF Maker is available as a free Android app on the Google Play Store, with more features than the web tool.

How many images can I add at once?

You can add up to 20 images in one go, and remove any of them before creating the PDF.
